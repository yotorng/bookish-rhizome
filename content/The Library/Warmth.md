Book by Daniel Sherrell

🌫️🍂🤜moved through august with a lot of climate anxiety and grief (i hesitate to turn every recent devastation into a running laundry list, and will decidedly Not...!), and Warmth was a precious little salve for the soft!! animal!! of my body!!! ❤️‍🩹😭🌱  
  
📮Half-memoir, half-letter to a hypothetical child, Warmth contends the weight of the climate crisis as it colours Daniel Sherrell’s life as a climate organiser. Not in its abstract forms of campaigning, legal negotiations and scientific reports, but in the manifold, deeply personal ways the Problem(as he coins it) makes itself *felt*. Through these tender letters, Sherrell attempts to open a line of communication across geologic eras as a way to process the immensity of it all. 🪨 And in doing so, Warmth is fundamentally an earnest endeavour in imagination, connection and care.  
  
After all...  
✋ to extend an outreached palm to the future is to also extend beyond the fleshy boundaries of an individual self, to acknowledge that our lives are ever-so-suspended in this tangled space-time tapestry🪢  
👣 it is not a fool’s errand to grasp tightly onto our capacity for hope and climate action. Despite how compromised our future seems (and the likelihood that things will definitely get worse😔), the climate crisis isn’t a binary, neat problem!! There lies a world of difference in the number of lives saved for every marginal reduction in global warming ; There remains a wealth of outcomes residing in this anthropogenic zone of uncertainties, and we still get to choose at every turn. ⛓️‍💥🪺🍄‍🟫  
💭 imagination and attention are two sides of the same coin. “Because what is imagination except a conjuring of the hypothetical, a line of attention spooled out beyond the senses?” In order to unsettle the present and untether ourselves from hubristic optimism + doomerism, we need to attune our attention!! To sit with our selves, to tread lightly, to observe deeply and curiously. imagination & attention as a vehicle,,,helping us move towards the horizon of our futures, whatever it holds, however we mold 🤲🌀🥾
